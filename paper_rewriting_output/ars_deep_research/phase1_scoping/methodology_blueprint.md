# Phase 1 — Methodology Blueprint

## Paradigm
Pragmatist / evidence-synthesis for computational protocol design.

## Method
1. Systematic bibliographic search (OpenAlex 2019–2026 + targeted ACS/JACS/JMC fetches).  
2. Source grading + thematic synthesis against the user’s Step0–7 SOP.  
3. Devil’s-advocate checkpoint on scientific rationality.  
4. Experiment-agent **plan** mode: produce a revised pre-docking protocol with success criteria.

## Inclusion (literature)
- Covalent VS / warhead enumeration / acrylamide libraries  
- Ligand-based vs scaffold-hop methods for kinases  
- AF3/covalent docking enrichment & role of prefilters  
- JNK Cys116 covalent inhibitors (YL5084, 56d, JNK-IN-8, reversible warheads)  
- Reactivity (GSH), active learning for VS, uncertainty/enrichment evaluation  

## Exclusion
- Non-peer / predatory when quality unclear  
- Off-topic biology reviews without methods relevance  
- SARS-CoV-2 docking spam unless methodologically transferable  

## Validity criteria for the *plan*
A plan is “rational” only if it specifies: (a) calibrated thresholds or sensitivity analysis, (b) warhead-aware similarity, (c) reactivity & selectivity controls, (d) falsifiable success metrics, (e) clear role of Glide vs AF3.
