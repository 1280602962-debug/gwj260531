## Controlling motivation (one paragraph)

Near-homologous JNK1/2/3 ATP sites (~98% identity) make cheap computational isoform-selectivity filters attractive as purchase criteria, yet those filters are rarely stress-tested on literature-calibrated JNK panels. The field therefore needs (i) **quantified failure evidence** that docking Δsel, Gly87 occupancy heuristics, and ML selectivity labels are **not** reliable purchase drivers for JNK isoform preference, and (ii) a still-practical **JNK-family binder enrichment pipeline** that can feed enzymatic IC50 boards without pretending those filters solved selectivity.

---

## What this motivation is *not*

- Not “we discovered a JNK1-selective clinical lead.”  
- Not “MD hinge occupancy proves isoform selectivity.”  
- Not “kinome-clean JNK inhibitors” (kinome not measured under wet-lab constraint).
