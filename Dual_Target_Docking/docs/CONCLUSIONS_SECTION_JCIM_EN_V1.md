# Conclusions (JCIM Articles draft, English)

## 5. Conclusions

If dual-target docking is evaluated only as Dual versus neither, single-target selectives can be overlooked; on the present four ChEMBL-derived pairs that formulation effect is pair-, chemistry-, and receptor-dependent. The EGFR/HER2 descriptive gap remained under independent GNINA pose generation, but it was not a four-pair law, all primary `summary_min` intervals included 0.5, and docking added at most 0.020 AUROC beyond scaffold-grouped ECFP4.

These findings identify evaluation failure modes within the frozen panels and protocols; they do not define a target-general reliability boundary, biological recognition, or external transfer. Dual-target virtual screening should therefore report directional hard negatives, ligand-only chemical controls, document- or panel-membership sensitivity, and receptor-realization checks before treating dual-pocket scores as dual-activity evidence. A pre-frozen BindingDB-native rebuild yielded zero pairs meeting the pre-frozen primary external gate and was not docked; a literature-year split is not claimed as external validation. MCL1/Bcl-xL is retained only as an exploratory archive after formal demotion of pose-gold status.
