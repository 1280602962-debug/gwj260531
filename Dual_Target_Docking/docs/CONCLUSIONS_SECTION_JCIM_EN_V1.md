# Conclusions (JCIM Articles draft, English)

## 5. Conclusions

This study established DualFourClass-Bench to evaluate directional dual-target docking against experimentally defined A-selective and B-selective hard negatives. Apparent discrimination was pair-dependent, often no stronger than ligand-property or fingerprint baselines, and sensitive to receptor choice.

These results indicate that favorable scores in both pockets are insufficient evidence of dual activity. Dual-target virtual screening, and generative workflows that use docking as a downstream filter, should therefore consider selective hard negatives, ligand-level chemical controls, unused ligand pools, and receptor-structure sensitivity together.
